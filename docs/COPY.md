# Notification copy (warm, playful, never guilt-tripping)
Placeholders: {name} {group} {km} {n} {rank}
- eligible_again: "Your blood has had {days} days to recharge. Ready to be somebody's hero again?"
- match_nearby: "{name}, someone {km} km away needs {group}. That's your type. Got 10 minutes?"
- thanks: "{requester}'s family says thank you. You just helped up to 3 people."
- rank_up: "{n} donations. You're now a {rank}."
- soft_snooze: "No pressure. We'll check back next month."
- welcome: "Welcome to BloodLine. One request, one room, one very happy family."
Never use: "Don't let someone down", "Your streak is dying", any shame or fear.
Streaks, if built, reward on-schedule donating only (never donating sooner than eligible).
